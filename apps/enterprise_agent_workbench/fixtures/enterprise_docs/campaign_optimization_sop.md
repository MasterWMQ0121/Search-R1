# Campaign Optimization Standard Operating Procedure

## ROI decline investigation

When return on investment declines, compare the most recent seven complete
days with the preceding seven days. Review spend, revenue, click-through rate,
conversion rate, channel mix, and refund signals before recommending a budget
change.

## Corrective actions

Prefer reversible actions. Check tracking quality and conversion efficiency,
shift spend only when the evidence supports it, document the expected outcome,
and schedule a follow-up review. Do not infer causality from a single aggregate
metric.

## Escalation

Escalate when the anomaly is unexplained, when data is incomplete, or when the
proposed change crosses a policy threshold. Pausing, resuming, and changing a
budget are auditable business writes.
