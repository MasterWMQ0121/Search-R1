# Advertising Budget Policy

## Standard increase threshold

A campaign daily-budget increase of no more than 10 percent from the current
approved budget may follow the standard operating review. Any larger increase
requires a manager's explicit approval and a recorded business justification.
The Enterprise Agent Workbench requires human approval for every write even
when the policy's standard threshold would otherwise apply.

## Absolute fixture limit

For this deterministic demonstration environment, a campaign daily budget must
not exceed 1500 currency units. A proposed value above that limit is
non-compliant and must not be executed.

## Required evidence

Budget-change recommendations must reference current campaign state, recent
performance data, and the applicable policy. The audit record must include the
requesting user, approved arguments, before state, and after state.
